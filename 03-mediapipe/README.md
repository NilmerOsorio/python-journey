# 🚀 MediaPipe — Computer Vision with Python

Welcome to my **MediaPipe learning journey!**

In this section of my Python journey, I'll explore computer vision using **MediaPipe, Python, and OpenCV**.

The goal is to learn by building real projects, experimenting with code, and understanding how things work under the hood — not just following tutorials or copying code.

Starting with hand detection, I'll gradually explore how computers can recognize hands, understand gestures, detect faces, track body movements, and identify objects.

## 🎯 What Am I Going to Learn?

Throughout this journey, I'll explore:

- 🧠 **MediaPipe Fundamentals:** Understand how MediaPipe works, its models, Tasks, and detection results.
- ✋ **Hand Detection:** Work with hand tracking, the 21 hand landmarks, and their coordinates.
- 👆 **Finger Counting & Gestures:** Build applications that recognize fingers and hand gestures.
- 📷 **Image & Video Processing:** Process images, videos, and real-time webcam streams.
- 😀 **Face Detection:** Detect faces and explore facial landmarks.
- 🧍 **Pose Estimation:** Detect body landmarks and track human movements.
- 🎯 **Object Detection:** Identify objects and understand their positions in images.
- 💡 **Practical Projects:** Combine everything I've learned to build interactive computer vision applications.
